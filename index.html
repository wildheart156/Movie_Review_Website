Critical Thinking Questions

01 — Cascade Investigation

I normally give each review box its background color with the .review selector. The original rule is background-color: #E1D697, which gives the review boxes their light yellow background.

For my cascade test, I removed background-color: #E1D697; from the .review rule. I also used the more specific #reviews .review selector as a second rule that supplies the same background color.

This shows that the final appearance is determined by the cascade: the browser considers both matching selectors, and the selector with greater specificity wins. In this test, source order is not needed to break the tie because the second selector already has higher specificity.
